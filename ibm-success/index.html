Who Is IBM — Built for Complex Government & Infrastructure

Why IBM for NCDOT?

IBM is a 112-year-old technology and consulting company that specializes in solving what others call "unsolvable" — deeply interconnected legacy systems, fragmented government data, and complex multi-agency infrastructure. IBM's core differentiator is its proven ability to modernize systems incrementally, without requiring agencies to rebuild everything at once or take critical services offline.

For NCDOT, IBM brings three critical capabilities: the technical depth to decouple data silos across ferries, highways, tolls, and transit; the government experience to work within regulatory and budget constraints; and a real-time AI platform (watsonx + Confluent) that transforms unified data into predictive, revenue-generating intelligence.

Government-Scale Complexity

Proven across IRS, VA, OPM, and dozens of federal agencies. IBM modernizes mission-critical government systems without service interruption.

Incremental Modernization

IBM's signature approach: deconstruct one subsystem at a time. No "big bang" rip-and-replace required. NCDOT can start with one modal silo and build outward.

AI-First Data Platform

IBM's acquisition of Confluent (2026) gives NCDOT real-time data streaming across all silos. IBM watsonx delivers AI for demand forecasting and dynamic pricing.
