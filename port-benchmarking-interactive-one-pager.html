Sources & Methodology Notes

Benchmark Scores: All scores (Throughput & Scale, Operational Efficiency, Infrastructure, Financial & Revenue, Connectivity, Governance) are internal benchmark ratings produced by this Container Port Benchmarking Research study. They are not independently validated external industry ratings. Each dimension is weighted equally (1/6); scores are averaged into an overall score on a 0–100 scale.

Ranking Note: Port of Savannah (89/100) and Port of Virginia (89/100) are tied overall; Savannah ranks #1 due to superior Throughput & Scale score. Rankings reflect scores at time of research.

Financial Data: Revenue and operating income figures are sourced from audited financial statements: SC Ports Authority FY2025 (year ended June 30, 2025); Virginia Port Authority FY2024 (year ended June 30, 2024); PANYNJ CY2024 (year ended December 31, 2024); Georgia Ports Authority FY2024/2025 (year ended June 30); Port Everglades FY2024 (year ended September 30, 2024); PortMiami FY2024 (year ended September 30, 2024). PANYNJ port-segment revenues are estimated; full-agency figures are not comparable to single-purpose port revenues. Port of Virginia and PortMiami total revenues include estimated figures derived from confirmed operating income and Fitch/S&P commentary — labeled "estimated" throughout.

Key Sources: BTS Port Performance Freight Statistics 2026 Annual Report (Jan 10, 2026); World Bank Container Port Performance Index 2020–2024 (Sep 2025); SC Ports Authority FY2025 Financial Statements; Virginia Port Authority FY2024 ACFR; PANYNJ Annual Financial Statements 2024; Georgia Ports Authority FY2025 ACFR; Broward County Port Everglades FY2024 Financial Statements; Fitch Ratings — PANYNJ AA- (Aug 6, 2025) and PortMiami A Stable (Aug 22, 2025); S&P Global — GPA Reaffirmation (Aug 19, 2025); ASCE 2025 Infrastructure Report Card.

Infrastructure Data: Channel depths, STS crane counts, and berth lengths are sourced from port press releases, BTS 2026 Annual Report, and individual port ACFRs. Virginia's 55-foot channel target was nearing completion as of January 2026. Savannah's channel deepening feasibility study was formally initiated June 2026.
